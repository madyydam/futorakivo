Indian Regulations & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance Manual

Official Statutory & Technical Guide for KIVOKO Restaurant Management & POS SaaS

Jurisdiction: Republic of India • Enforcement: 2026 Enterprise Statutory Baseline

Scope: This document outlines every mandatory legal, data protection, food safety, consumer protection, and fiscal tax compliance rule required to operate a Restaurant POS, Table QR self-ordering system, and customer CRM in India.

1. Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)

Under the DPDP Act 2023 (enacted by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology - MeitY):

  • Data Fiduciary: The Restaurant business entity collecting customer phone and name.
  • Data Processor: KIVOKO (Futora) SaaS hosting and processing the multi-tenant data.
  • Data Principal: The dining customer / guest scanning the QR code or providing contact details.

Section 6: Explicit, Unbundled Consent (Mandatory Unchecked Checkbox)

Under Section 6(1), consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous with clear affirmative action.

STRICTLY PROHIBITED: Pre-checked boxes, bundled marketing opt-ins, or forcing customers to surrender personal data for dine-in service are illegal under Indian law.
  • Checkbox State: The Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy checkbox on the QR menu landing screen must be UNCHECKED by default.
  • Proof of Consent: Every consent tick must record an immutable audit row: Customer ID, UTC Timestamp, Client IP Address, Browser User-Agent, and Terms Policy Version.

Statutory Penalties for DPDP Act Breaches (Section 33 & Schedule)

Violation Category Governing Section Maximum Penalty
Failure to implement reasonable security safeguards against data breaches Section 8(5) / Schedule 1 Up to ₹250 Crores
Failure to notify Data Protection Board of personal data breach Section 8(6) / Schedule 2 Up to ₹200 Crores
Non-observance of obligations regarding children or vulnerable data Section 9 / Schedule 3 Up to ₹200 Crores
Failure to fulfill general Fiduciary obligations (Consent, Notice, Erasure) Schedule 4 Up to ₹50 Crores

3-Hour Customer Screen Auto-Expiry (Privacy Shield)

To prevent subsequent guests or unauthorized third parties from viewing a previous party's order history, live order tracking on mobile web (/m/:qrToken/status) must automatically expire 3 hours post-creation.

2. Food Safety & Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)

  • Mandatory 14-Digit License Display: FSSAI Order dated 8th June 2021 mandates printing the 14-digit FSSAI License Number on all physical printed bills, WhatsApp e-receipts, and the digital QR menu.
  • Veg / Non-Veg Declarations: Green filled circle inside green square outline for Vegetarian food; Brown filled triangle inside brown square outline for Non-Vegetarian items containing meat/egg.
  • Allergen Warnings: Display of the 8 statutory allergens (Gluten, Crustaceans, Eggs, Fish, Peanuts/Tree Nuts, Soya, Milk/Lactose, Sulphites).

3. Goods & Services Tax (GST) & Invoicing Compliance

  • SAC Code 9963: Restaurant and food & beverage services.
  • 5% GST (No ITC): Standalone restaurants charge 2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST without Input Tax Credit.
  • Mandatory Rule 46 Fields: Consecutive invoice numbering per financial year, Restaurant GSTIN, SAC code, customer B2B GSTIN (when requested), and itemized tax breakdown.
  • Section 170 CGST Act: Total bill and tax rounding to the nearest Rupee.

4. CCPA Guidelines on Service Charges (2022)

  • Zero Mandatory Service Charges: As per Central Consumer Protection Authority guidelines, restaurants cannot automatically add service charge by default.
  • Strictly Voluntary: Any tip or gratuity must be explicit, voluntary, and easily removable by the guest.

5. TRAI / DLT Regulations for SMS & WhatsApp

  • Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT): Principal Entity registration and 6-character Header (Sender ID) registration.
  • Template Approval: All transactional order updates, OTPs, and bill PDF messages must use pre-approved telecom templates.
