Executive Summary

This report provides the definitive architectural blueprint and feature definition manual documenting the transformation of KIVO from a local self-hosted restaurant POS script into a centralized, highly scalable Multi-Tenant Cloud SaaS platform directly matching the industry capabilities of Petpooja. Over 10 phased milestones, KIVO has achieved multi-tenant cloud database isolation, zero-touch trial signups, platform-wide super admin control, automated recurring licensing with UPI QR checkout, hybrid offline-first POS reliability, browser-to-hardware ESC/POS bridge, delivery aggregator relay, central kitchen supply chain indents, and high-concurrency cloud containerization.

Phase-by-Phase Execution & Feature Definition Matrix

Phase 1: Central Cloud Database Architecture Completed
Main Goal: Shift from individual local Docker installations to a central high-availability multi-tenant PostgreSQL database.
  • Central Cloud PostgreSQL: All 10,000+ restaurants reside on a unified cloud DB engine with high concurrency connection pooling (PgBouncer).
  • Automated Schema Provisioning: Zero local DB maintenance for restaurant operators; automatic schema migrations via Prisma.
  • Zero-Hardware Dependency: Restaurants no longer need to purchase expensive on-premise local servers.
Phase 2: Multi-Tenant SaaS Hardening & Data Isolation Completed
Main Goal: Complete data segregation across restaurant brands with strict tenant boundaries.
  • Strict Tenant Scoping: Every query, order, customer, and KOT is strictly tied to restaurantId and branchId.
  • Multi-Brand Privacy: Tenant A cannot inspect, modify, or leak data into Tenant B.
  • Role & Privilege Enforcement: Granular permissions across Owner, Administrator, Cashier, Waiter, and Kitchen staff.
Phase 3: Instant Self-Serve Signup & Onboarding Flow Completed
Main Goal: Zero-touch public SaaS registration directly from the marketing website.
  • Public Signup Unblocking: ALLOW_PUBLIC_SIGNUP unblocked to allow instant self-serve restaurant creation.
  • Automatic 14-Day Free Trial: New signups are provisioned on the Starter plan with a 14-day trial without requiring upfront credit cards.
  • Instant Branch & Tax Preset: New restaurants automatically initialize with regional GST / tax presets and a primary branch.
Phase 4: Super Admin Platform Control Console (/super-admin) Completed
Main Goal: Platform-wide monitoring and control console for FUTORA group operators.
  • Tenant Aggregation Directory: Search and filter all registered restaurant brands across Active, Trial, and Suspended states.
  • Live Platform Analytics: 30-day GMV, active outlet volume, and recurring SaaS MRR estimates.
  • Instant Impersonation Engine: One-click "Impersonate" to log into any tenant's dashboard as Owner to diagnose support requests.
  • Global Platform Broadcasts: Dispatch system announcements and maintenance notices directly to all POS screens.
  • Responsive Floating Sidebar: Dedicated SaaS navigation sidebar with mobile drawer support.
Phase 5: SaaS Subscription, Licensing & Payment Gateway Engine (/settings/billing) Completed
Main Goal: Automated tier-based subscription licensing with instant UPI and card checkout.
  • 3-Tier Subscription Plans:
    • Starter Plan (₹599/mo | ₹5,999/yr): 1 Outlet, unlimited cloud billing, UPI settlements, basic inventory.
    • Growth Plan (₹1,499/mo | ₹14,999/yr): Up to 3 Outlets, direct thermal printing, recipe costing, staff permissions.
    • Enterprise Plan (₹3,999/mo | ₹39,999/yr): Unlimited Outlets, central kitchen, API webhooks, 24/7 SLA.
  • Annual Discount Switcher: 2 Months Free (17% savings) toggle for annual billings.
  • Instant Payment Gateway Simulator: Integrated Razorpay / Dynamic UPI QR modal with automatic 18% GST calculation.
  • GST Tax Invoices: Automatic generation of compliant GST tax invoices (INV-KIVO-YYYY-XXXXX) with PDF download actions.
  • SubscriptionGuard (License Enforcement): Restricts write actions and bill creation when trial expires, allowing read-only historical reports until renewed.
Phase 6: Petpooja-Style Hybrid Offline-First POS (Outbox Pattern) Completed
Main Goal: Counter billing never stops even if broadband or Wi-Fi is severed.
  • Client Offline Outbox (lib/offline-outbox.ts): Orders and bill settlements are captured into encrypted local storage under #OFF-xxx temporary IDs.
  • Automatic Background Cloud Sync: Real-time listeners trigger FIFO batch flushing to cloud backend the moment internet reconnects.
  • Live Sync Status Bar (OfflineSyncIndicator): Reassuring green pill ("Cloud Synced") / amber pulsing indicator ("Offline POS (X queued)") in header with an interactive sync drawer.
Phase 7: Thermal Printer & Hardware Integration Bridge (/settings/hardware) Completed
Main Goal: Browser-to-hardware communication for 80mm/58mm thermal printers, cash drawers, and audio soundboxes.
  • Web Serial & Web Bluetooth ESC/POS: Direct binary ESC/POS printing from counter PC/tablet to USB and Bluetooth printers without third-party drivers.
  • Universal Cash Drawer Kick: Standard RJ11 pulse (ESC p 0 25 250) on cash bill settlement.
  • Web Audio Soundbox Voice Chimes: Multi-tone harmonic chime + vocal speech announcement ("New order received on KIVO: Table X, ₹450") without external MP3 files.
  • Hardware Diagnostics Hub: Real-time sample bill printing, KOT ticket printing, and auto-cut paper toggles.
Phase 8: Food Aggregator & Delivery Integrations (/integrations/aggregators) Completed
Main Goal: Centralized two-way relay between online delivery platforms, POS billing, and kitchen screens.
  • Aggregator Inbound Webhooks: Normalized receiver endpoints for Zomato, Swiggy, ONDC, and Magicpin.
  • Real-Time KDS Relay: Aggregator orders automatically route to kitchen screens and thermal printers with customer notes and rider info.
  • One-Click Order Workflow: "Accept Order (20m)", "Mark Food Ready (Alert Rider)", and "Handover (Dispatched)".
  • Item 86 (Out-of-Stock) Instant Switcher: Toggle any dish on/off; immediately syncs across Swiggy and Zomato customer apps.
  • Simulation Suite: One-click simulation of incoming Zomato / Swiggy orders to test live soundbox alerts and kitchen tickets.
Phase 9: Multi-Branch & Central Kitchen Supply Chain Hub Completed
Main Goal: End-to-end multi-outlet supply chain, commissary kitchen indents, cold-chain logistics, and brand-wide executive analytics.
  • Central Kitchen / Commissary Designation: Multi-outlet brands can mark any location as a Central Kitchen / master warehouse (isCentralKitchen: true) that supplies regional dining outlets.
  • Requisition & Stock Indent Engine (StockIndent & StockIndentItem): Requisition lifecycle with strict state transitions (PENDING → APPROVED → DISPATCHED → RECEIVED → REJECTED) and urgency classification (NORMAL, HIGH, CRITICAL).
  • Cold-Chain Dispatch & Automated Stock Consumption: Dispatches log delivery van number (dispatchVehicle) and temperature/dispatch notes, while atomically drawing down stock from the Central Kitchen warehouse.
  • Stock Inbound Verification & Shrinkage Control: Destination stores physically verify and input received counts; automatically creates weighted-average cost batches (StockBatch) and logs RECEIPT movements into outlet inventory.
  • Consolidated Multi-Outlet Executive Hub (/reports/consolidated): Brand-level reporting aggregating gross sales, order volume, AOV, individual outlet performance benchmarking matrix, cross-outlet top-selling items, and supply chain health indicators with CSV export.
Phase 10: Production Cloud Deployment, CI/CD & Launch Completed
Main Goal: Production Cloud SaaS infrastructure, containerized high-concurrency scaling, CI/CD pipeline, and zero-downtime deployment automation.
  • High-Concurrency PgBouncer Pooling: Dedicated transaction-mode connection pooling (pgbouncer:6432) capable of servicing 10,000+ simultaneous restaurant POS terminals and kitchen screens against central PostgreSQL.
  • Production Containerization & Health Probes: Multi-stage production Dockerfiles with Alpine minimal footprints and automated live/ready health checks (/health, /ready) reporting DB latency and memory usage.
  • Automated TLS/SSL Edge Proxy (Caddy): Automatic Let's Encrypt SSL/TLS termination, HTTP/2 & HTTP/3 multiplexing, Brotli compression, and security headers.
  • GitHub Actions CI/CD Pipeline: Automated end-to-end quality gates (typechecks across packages, 59 unit tests), multi-architecture container build, and deployment triggers.
  • Disaster Recovery & Point-In-Time Backups: Automated S3/R2 encrypted offsite cloud backup routines for tenant peace of mind.
